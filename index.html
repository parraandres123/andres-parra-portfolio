Andrés Parra Sánchez
Andrés Parra Sánchez

Hello, my name is Andrés, and I am from Bogotá, Colombia.

I like to see design as a way to give meaning to the everyday, to transform ideas into realities.

In my projects, I always seek a balance between the functional, the aesthetic, and the conceptual.

It is important to me that my work carries a deeper significance, whether social, environmental, or cultural.

I like to think that design does more than just solve problems; it can also tell stories, create an impact, and forge genuine connections with people.

I truly enjoy experimenting with forms, materials, and visual references. I draw inspiration from my surroundings and try to ensure that each proposal has something special, something that says more than what meets the eye.
